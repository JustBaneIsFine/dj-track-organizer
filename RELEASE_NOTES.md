## v0.1.4.4

- After an update finishes you can open everything it found with "Show all new tracks", instead of losing the list the moment you click into one artist.
- Update results are saved. Reopen a past update from the Update menu under "Recent results", and choose how many to keep in Settings (default 5).
- Tracks you check off leave the run's list, and the run disappears once nothing is left in it.
- Fixed: clicking "update this artist" on several artists in a row now queues them all, instead of the last click replacing the ones before it.

100% local: no accounts, no telemetry. Provided as is, use at your own risk. Not affiliated with SoundCloud.
