## v0.1.4.3

- Added a "Priority + most to do" sort for artists: highest priority first, and within each priority level the ones with the most to do first.
- "Update this artist" now queues: clicking it on several artists in a row scrapes them one after another (with a live queue count) instead of only updating the last one.

100% local: no accounts, no telemetry. Provided as is, use at your own risk. Not affiliated with SoundCloud.
